Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Acqua Nails Spa ("we," "our," or "the salon") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website (acquanailspa.netlify.app), message us through Google, Messenger, or other chat channels, or book an appointment, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, phone number, and email address when you book an appointment, send us a message, or buy a gift certificate.
  • Message content: the questions and information you send us in chat conversations, so we can answer you.
  • Basic technical data: our website host may automatically record standard information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and remind you about appointments.
  • To answer your questions about services, prices, and availability.
  • To complete purchases such as gift certificates.
  • Occasionally, and only if you agree, to send you news about promotions or seasonal offers.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

We use platforms such as Google and Meta (Messenger) to receive and reply to your messages, and a point-of-sale system to manage appointments. These providers process your information only as needed to deliver those services.

Keeping your information

We keep appointment and message records only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal or accounting requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices

  • You may ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • You may opt out of promotional messages at any time by telling us in the chat, calling the salon, or replying STOP to a text message.

Children

Our website and chat are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is always the current one, and the "Last updated" date above will change when we revise it.
